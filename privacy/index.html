Pullscope Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-12

Scan a trading card to see what it is and what it's worth, and keep your collection on your iPhone. This policy explains what data Pullscope uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

In short

  • Your collection and your searches are stored only on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync. Card photos leave your device only after you allow cloud recognition, and only to identify the card.
  • No ads. No tracking across apps or websites. We don't sell your data.

Cloud card recognition (needs your permission)

Before the first scan, Pullscope shows a screen titled “Identify cards with cloud recognition”. Only if you tap Allow & scan does the app send the photo of the card you scan to identify it. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Cloud card recognition; the app will ask again before sending another photo. Scanning needs an internet connection; search and your collection work offline.

Who receives it

  • Ximilar s.r.o. (Czech Republic, EU) — Identifies the trading card in your photo. This is the main recognition service. Receives: the photo of the card you scan (cropped, with all EXIF and location data removed) and the games you chose. Inference images are processed in memory and not stored. Ximilar keeps security and API access logs for 12 months. Processing happens on EU infrastructure under the GDPR, with Ximilar acting as a data processor for Pullscope. Privacy policy · source · source
  • OpenAI (USA) — Identifies the card only when Ximilar cannot recognise it or is unavailable. Receives: the same card photo (cropped, without EXIF or location data). Data sent to the OpenAI API is not used to train or improve OpenAI models. OpenAI keeps abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days, longer only where the law requires it or to protect its services or others from harm. Privacy policy · source · source
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Remote Config) — Analytics tells us which features are used and where errors happen, so we can fix them. Remote Config delivers the app's settings. Never used for advertising or cross-app tracking. Receives: in-app usage events (for example: screen opened, card scanned, card added, Pro screen viewed, error shown), device model, iOS version, app version and app-generated identifiers. Privacy policy
  • Pullscope data host (GitHub Pages) — Delivers the card catalog, prices and Yu-Gi-Oh! card images to the app. Receives: your IP address and basic request information, as with any download. Privacy policy
  • Scryfall — Serves Magic: The Gathering card images shown in the app. Receives: your IP address and basic request information when an image is downloaded. Privacy policy
  • TCGdex — Serves Pokémon card images shown in the app. Receives: your IP address and basic request information when an image is downloaded. Privacy policy
  • OPTCG API — Serves One Piece card images shown in the app. Receives: your IP address and basic request information when an image is downloaded. About (no separate privacy policy published)
  • Lorcast — Serves Disney Lorcana card images shown in the app. Receives: your IP address and basic request information when an image is downloaded. About (no separate privacy policy published)
  • Apple (App Store / StoreKit) — Handles the Pullscope Pro purchase. We never see your payment details. Receives: purchase and subscription status. Privacy policy

How third parties protect your data

We send card photos only to the recognition providers listed above, only to identify the card. Each of them processes the photo under its published terms for business customers, which protect it at the same level as this policy or higher: Ximilar does not store the photo and processes it in the EU under the GDPR; OpenAI does not use API data to train its models and keeps it for at most 30 days for abuse monitoring. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

What stays on your device

  • Your collection: cards, variant, condition, quantity and the price you paid.
  • Your recent searches, the games you chose and your daily scan count.
  • Text read from the card on your device, used to confirm the match.
  • Card photos are not kept. After you allow cloud recognition, each photo is sent directly to the recognition service only to identify the card. Pullscope does not save it.

What we don't collect

  • Your name, email address or any account — the app has no sign-in.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA), location, contacts or browsing history.
  • Photo location or EXIF data — it is removed before a card photo is sent.

Permissions

  • Camera — To photograph your trading cards. After you allow cloud recognition, the card photo is sent to Ximilar (and to OpenAI only if Ximilar cannot identify it) to identify the card. You can also pick a photo instead with the iOS photo picker, which needs no library permission.

Your choices

  • Turn off cloud card recognition in Settings → Privacy → Cloud card recognition. Pullscope will ask again before the next scan sends a photo.
  • Revoke camera access in iOS Settings → Pullscope.
  • Delete a card from your collection on the card's edit screen.
  • Remove a game's downloaded catalog in Settings → Games.
  • Delete all your data by deleting the app.

Purchases

Pullscope Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your card details. Restore purchases in Settings → Pullscope Pro → Restore Purchases, or on the Pro screen.

Children

Pullscope is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
