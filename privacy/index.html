Pullscope Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-13

Scan a trading card to see what it is and what it's worth, and keep your collection on your iPhone. This policy explains what data Pullscope uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

In short

  • Your collection, portfolios, wishlist, profile and searches are stored only on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync. Card photos leave your device only after you allow cloud recognition, and only to identify or grade the card.
  • No ads inside the app. We don't sell your data. With your permission (Apple's tracking prompt), we use your device's advertising identifier only to measure which ads brought you to Pullscope.

Advertising measurement and tracking

Pullscope shows no ads, but we run ads for Pullscope elsewhere and measure them with Google. After you start using the app, iOS asks whether Pullscope may track you. Only if you tap Allow does Google's SDK read your device's advertising identifier (IDFA); until then, and if you decline, advertising consent stays off. Separately, Google Ads on-device conversion measurement uses short-lived, anonymous event data (such as IP address and time) on your device to measure our iOS ad campaigns without identifying you; it does not run in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Cloud card recognition (needs your permission)

Before the first scan, Pullscope shows a screen titled “Identify cards with cloud recognition”. Only if you tap Allow & scan does the app send the photo of the card you scan to identify it. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Cloud card recognition; the app will ask again before sending another photo. Scanning and loading new card data need an internet connection; your collection and card data you have already viewed work offline.

Who receives it

  • Ximilar s.r.o. (Czech Republic, EU) — Identifies the trading card in your photo, and estimates a card's condition when you use AI Grading (Pullscope Pro). Receives: the photo of the card you scan (cropped, with all EXIF and location data removed) and the games you chose; for AI Grading, the front and back photos you take or choose. Inference images are processed in memory and not stored. Ximilar keeps security and API access logs for 12 months. Processing happens on EU infrastructure under the GDPR, with Ximilar acting as a data processor for Pullscope. Privacy policy · source · source
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Remote Config) — Analytics tells us which features are used and where errors happen. Remote Config delivers the app's settings. Analytics is linked to Google Ads to measure our ad campaigns. Receives: in-app usage events (for example: screen opened, card scanned, card added, Pro screen viewed, error shown), device model, iOS version, app version and app-generated identifiers. Privacy policy
  • Google Ads (on-device conversion measurement) — Measures which of our iOS ad campaigns lead to installs and actions, using anonymous, temporary event data processed on your device. Not used in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland. Receives: anonymous, temporary event data such as IP address and time; your advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allowed tracking. Privacy policy
  • Scryfall — Provides Magic: The Gathering card details, prices and images. Receives: your IP address and the card name or ID you search for or open. Privacy policy
  • YGOPRODeck — Provides Yu-Gi-Oh! card details and prices. The app does not load Yu-Gi-Oh! card images; it shows the card name and set code instead. Receives: your IP address and the card name or set code you search for or open. Privacy policy
  • TCGdex — Provides Pokémon card details, prices and images (English and Japanese). Receives: your IP address and the card name or ID you search for or open. Privacy policy
  • OPTCG API — Provides One Piece card details, prices and images. Receives: your IP address and the card code or set you search for or open. About (no separate privacy policy published)
  • Lorcast — Provides Disney Lorcana card details, prices and images. Receives: your IP address and the card name or set you search for or open. About (no separate privacy policy published)
  • European Central Bank — Provides daily exchange rates so prices can be shown in your currency. Converted prices are estimates. Receives: your IP address when the app downloads the daily rates (about once a day). Privacy policy
  • Apple (App Store / StoreKit) — Handles the Pullscope Pro purchase. We never see your payment details. Receives: purchase and subscription status. Privacy policy

How third parties protect your data

We share the card photos you take only with Ximilar, which processes them to identify the card or to estimate its condition with AI Grading, and which provides the same or equal protection of your data as described in this policy. Ximilar does not store the photo and processes it in the EU under the GDPR. We do not sell your data.

What stays on your device

  • Your collection, portfolios, wishlist, showcase and value history.
  • Your profile (name, bio, avatar) and AI Grading reports.
  • Your recent searches, favourite games, notifications and daily scan count.
  • Card details, prices and images you have viewed, cached so the app works faster and offline.
  • Text read from the card on your device, used to confirm the match.
  • Card photos are not kept by Pullscope. After you allow cloud recognition, each photo is sent directly to the recognition service only to identify or grade the card.

What we don't collect

  • Your email address, phone number or any account — the app has no sign-in. Your profile stays on your device.
  • Your location, contacts or browsing history.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA), unless you allow tracking in Apple's prompt.
  • Photo location or EXIF data — it is removed before a card photo is sent.

Permissions

  • Camera — To photograph your cards for scanning and AI Grading, and to take a profile photo. A card photo leaves your device only after you allow cloud recognition. You can also pick a photo with the iOS photo picker, which needs no library permission.
  • Notifications — Only if you tap "Enable notifications": price-drop alerts for cards on your wishlist. Notifications are created on your device; we don't send push messages from a server.

Your choices

  • Turn off cloud card recognition in Settings → Privacy → Cloud card recognition. Pullscope will ask again before the next scan sends a photo.
  • Allow or stop tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Revoke camera or notification access in iOS Settings → Pullscope.
  • Delete a card from your collection on the card's edit screen.
  • Clear cached card data in Settings → Data & prices → Clear cache.
  • Delete all your data by deleting the app.

Purchases

Pullscope Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your card details. Restore purchases in Settings → Pullscope Pro → Restore Purchases, or on the Pro screen.

Children

Pullscope is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
