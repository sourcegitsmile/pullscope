How do I delete my data?

Your data is only on your iPhone. Delete a single card on its edit screen, clear cached card data in Settings → Data & prices → Clear cache, or delete the app to remove everything.

Do my card photos get uploaded?

Only after you tap "Allow & scan". Each card photo is sent to Ximilar to identify the card. EXIF and location data are removed first, and Pullscope does not store the photo. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy → Cloud card recognition.

Does scanning work offline?

No. Identifying a card and loading new card data need an internet connection. Your collection and card data you have already viewed are available offline.

Where do prices come from?

Prices are TCGplayer market prices in USD, provided by each game's card database (TCGdex, Scryfall, YGOPRODeck, OPTCG API, Lorcast). Each price shows its source and date. Prices in other currencies are converted with European Central Bank rates and are estimates, not offers to buy or sell.

How do I restore Pullscope Pro on a new iPhone?

Open Settings → Pullscope Pro → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Which games are supported?

Pokémon (English and Japanese), Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece and Disney Lorcana. Yu-Gi-Oh! cards are shown with their name and set code instead of an image.

Is AI Grading an official grade?

No. AI Grading is an estimate made by AI from your photos. It is not an official grade from PSA, BGS or any other grading company.
